About Barbara

A systems thinker. A builder. A leader who chose to bet on what's next.

Portrait Placeholder

Secondary image — working shot, on stage, or candid.

Barbara Salami spent twenty-plus years leading commercial innovation and digital transformation at the world's largest pharmaceutical and biotech organizations — GSK, Moderna, Novo Nordisk, AstraZeneca, and Bristol Myers Squibb. In May 2022, an early encounter with generative AI reshaped how she saw the future of leadership, work, and human judgment. By 2024, she had stepped out of the corporate path to build what she believed leaders actually needed.

Today, Barbara is the founder and CEO of KAINDLY, an AI fluency and adoption accelerator. She is also building Decyd, a biopharma strategic intelligence platform, and ExecIntel, an executive intelligence briefing practice. She serves on multiple AI advisory boards, keynotes industry conferences, and co-founded STEM Dragons — an afterschool coding and robotics program rooted in her conviction that AI fluency is an access issue, not a skills gap.

"The future belongs to those willing to learn before required to."
